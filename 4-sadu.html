Design system 04 of 04

Sadu

A circle of people who know each other by name. The colours and the one pattern come from Al Sadu, the Bedouin weaving of Qatar: madder red, black and ivory wool, saffron, with a sawtooth band along the edges. Faces come first, numbers second.

Feels
Warm brotherhood and sisterhood. Friendly, encouraging, a little playful.
Islamic identity
Rich, but cultural. Gulf textile heritage instead of mosque geometry. The woven band runs along hero cards, avatars and the absence mark.
Drawn from
Al Sadu weaving (UNESCO-listed); the painted doors of Souq Waqif; Headspace and Duolingo for tone and tactile buttons.
Gives up
Seriousness and density. It can feel too casual for approvals and audit logs, and the chunky shapes fit less on screen.

Screens

Sample screens built from docs/features. The countdown is live. Buttons have a pressed "felt" edge; try clicking one.

PressMe

Felt, not glass

Raised things have a solid bottom edge (2–6px, a darker shade of their own colour), and they sink when pressed. No blur shadows, no transparency. Radius: chips are pills; buttons, tiles and avatars are 14–18px; cards 22–28px.

The weave

The sawtooth band appears on the edges of hero cards, the verse block and avatars. Attendance history is "woven" as rows of coloured squares, one per Friday. That's the whole ornament vocabulary.

120ms press · 200ms slide
no confetti, no bounce

Warm, not hyper

Copy speaks to people by name ("Bilal is presenting", "Alhamdulillah, you made it"). Motion is limited to presses and slides. A late check-in is never shamed: saffron, not red.

Components

Statuses use shape as well as colour: on time is a filled square, pending is a ring, absent is woven black.

Rules

Do

  • Lead with people: a face or a name before a number, wherever a person is involved.
  • Keep madder red for "this Friday" and "you": the hero card, the active tab, your row.
  • Write copy that addresses people by name and sounds like a friend in the circle.
  • Use the woven squares for any per-Friday history.
  • Give every pressable thing a solid bottom edge.

Don't

  • Don't use red for lateness or errors. Late is saffron, absent is black wool, validation errors are madder text only.
  • Don't put the sawtooth band on more than one element per screen.
  • Don't use blur shadows, gradients or glass.
  • Don't use emoji as icons or for celebration.
  • Don't make the leaderboard a podium. It's a record, not a race.

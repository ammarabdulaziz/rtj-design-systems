Sahar

The last hour before Fajr: when the class wakes up, makes wudu and drives in. The whole app sits on that sky. It's deep night at the top, with the horizon warming toward first light at the bottom. Native components float on it as soft, hairline-edged glass.

Feels
Quiet and hopeful. Early morning, the drive to class, the light coming up.
Islamic identity
Through time, not pattern. The sky gradient is the identity. Arabic appears where it belongs (Quran, Bismillah), and Eid gets a crescent. No geometric wallpaper.
From the reference
Atmospheric gradient backgrounds, hairline glass cards, a serif for headings over a crisp sans, native segmented controls, circular nav buttons, filled tab icons.
Gives up
Some legibility headroom. Glass on gradients needs discipline, and it's dark-first by nature. Light mode is "Ishraq", the sky after sunrise.

Screens

Six screens from docs/features. The countdown is live, the scan line moves, and the segmented controls and switches work. Flip Dark/Light at the top.

On time

Glass, hairline, pill

Cards are 6% glass with a 1px hairline: no shadow, no blur halo. Radii: chips and buttons are pills, list cards 22px, hero 26px, sheets 34px. Circular 40px buttons for navigation.

The rising sun

The sun disc in the hero is the only illustration. It sits low on the horizon. On class morning (scan window open) it can rise into view. That's a nice moment, and a cheap one.

200ms ease-out · sheets 380ms
scan line 2.6s, respects reduced motion

Calm motion

Switches slide, sheets rise, the scan line sweeps. Numbers change in place. No parallax, no fade-ups, no confetti.

Components

Native shapes (switch, segmented control, sheet, grouped list) dressed in the system's glass.

Rules

Do

  • Put every screen on the sky gradient, fixed to the viewport so it doesn't scroll away.
  • Use "first light" for the one primary action and the selected state. Nothing decorative is accent-coloured.
  • Keep cards as glass plus a hairline. Separate rows inside them with hairlines too.
  • Use Cormorant only at 20px and up: titles, countdowns, quotes. Everything you read at small sizes is Manrope.
  • Give the dawn gradient to the next session only.

Don't

  • Don't stack glass on glass more than once. One layer on the sky, and that's it.
  • Don't add drop shadows to cards. Only sheets and dragged rows cast one.
  • Don't use the sun/sky as decoration elsewhere (empty states, onboarding art).
  • Don't put text directly on the brightest part of the horizon without checking contrast.
  • Don't add geometric patterns. In this system, time is the ornament.

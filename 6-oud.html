Design system 06

Oud

The majlis after class: dark wood, brass incense burners, the smell of oud. Warm espresso surfaces, a single brass accent, and a hero card cast in brass and engraved with an eight-fold girih star. It's luxurious, but it stays quiet.

Feels
Premium and dignified. Serious enough for approvals and audits, warm enough for a brotherhood.
Islamic identity
Crafted, restrained. A faint girih lattice is engraved into brass surfaces only (hero, ticket, Eid). The presenter banner shows arched windows. Arabic is set in Amiri Quran.
From the reference
Closest to it: espresso + brass, outlined tiles with a corner glow, a ticket-style QR with a brass scan line, pill duo buttons, uppercase button labels, filled tab icons.
Gives up
Brightness and lightness of touch. It's moody by design, and the light mode is a secondary "parchment" variant.

Screens

Six screens from docs/features. The countdown is live, the scan line moves, and the segmented controls and switches work.

Solid, hairline, outlined

Cards are solid ebony with a 1px warm hairline. They aren't glass, which keeps text crisp on the moody ground. Radii: tiles 16, cards 18–22, chips 8, buttons pills. Status marks are small rotated diamonds.

Brass is a material

The brass gradient (with its girih engraving) is reserved for objects you'd hold: the next session, the check-in ticket, Eid. Flat brass is for actions. Brass never appears as a large flat fill.

Rules

Do

  • Give every screen the two ambient brass glows on espresso. Never a flat background.
  • Use engraved brass for the next session, the check-in ticket and Eid. Nothing else.
  • Keep cards solid with a hairline, and add a soft brass glow in one corner for tappable tiles.
  • Set titles in Marcellus. Numbers, and everything you read or tap, go in Geist (Marcellus's zero reads as an O). Buttons are uppercase and tracked.
  • Show turns this cycle wherever staff make a rotation decision.

Don't

  • Don't spread the girih pattern beyond brass surfaces. One engraving per screen.
  • Don't use gold gradients on text, icons or borders.
  • Don't use glass blur. Surfaces are solid wood, not frosted.
  • Don't add a second accent. Status colours are muted and only appear on status marks.
  • Don't use stock mosque photography. The arches are drawn.

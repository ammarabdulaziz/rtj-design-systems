Design system 01 of 04

Mushaf

The app as a well-printed book. Every screen is set like a page of the Madinah Mushaf: cream leaf, naskh-black ink, a green-and-gilt frame, and the ayah rosette as the one recurring ornament.

Feels
Scholarly and reverent. Like a study circle with good books on the table.
Islamic identity
Rich. Double-rule frames, surah cartouche, 8-point rosette for numbers. All of it comes from the mushaf itself. No generic arabesque wallpaper.
Drawn from
The King Fahd Complex print of the Madinah Mushaf; manuscript rubrication (red ink for warnings); book-index leaders for the leaderboard.
Gives up
Speed and density. Stats read like a ledger, not a scoreboard. Serif UI is slower to scan for quick taps.

Shape

Frames, not shadows

Radius is 0 for cards and 2px for buttons. Toggles are the only round thing. A 3px double rule marks a "page" (a primary object); 1px hairlines separate rows. No drop shadows, except one on a dragged row.

Ornament

Three ornaments, total

The 8-point rosette holds a number (week, ayah). The cartouche holds a surah name. The gilt diamond marks the active tab and list bullets. Gilt is never used for text.

Space & motion

4px base, slow turns

Screen margin is 20px. Sections are 26px apart, rows 12px. Motion is limited to sheets rising (260ms, decelerate) and toggles (120ms). Lists, cards and numbers never animate in.

IV

Rules

For anyone building a screen this page doesn't show.

Do

  • Use Mushaf green for the one primary action on a screen, and nowhere else.
  • Put numbers that identify something (week, ayah) inside the rosette; put counts in plain lining figures.
  • Set people's names and portions in the serif; set metadata, times and labels in Plex Condensed.
  • Show Arabic in Amiri Quran, right-to-left, centered inside a framed block.
  • Use rubric red for anything that costs points.

Don't

  • Don't add geometric pattern backgrounds. The ornament budget is rosette, cartouche, diamond.
  • Don't use gilt for text or for interactive elements; it fails contrast and cheapens the ornament.
  • Don't round corners on cards or add shadows to make something "pop". Use the double frame.
  • Don't put more than one framed page per screen. Frame the most important object only.
  • Don't animate numbers counting up.

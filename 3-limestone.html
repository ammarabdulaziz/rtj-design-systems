Design system 03 of 04

Limestone

Quiet, like the courtyard of the Museum of Islamic Art at dawn. Pale stone, deep cut shadows, and one colour taken from the Corniche water. It behaves like a native phone app, and the only ornament is the shape of the arch.

Feels
Calm and reverent. Opening it should feel like walking into the masjid after Fajr.
Islamic identity
Subtle, structural. The next-session card is a mihrab arch. Successful check-in gets an 8-point khatam seal. Nothing else.
Drawn from
I. M. Pei's Museum of Islamic Art, Doha; the dawn tone on its limestone; Apple's own apps (Things 3, Health) for behaviour.
Gives up
Drama and brand recall. It's the most familiar of the four, and stats are deliberately understated.

Screens

Platform-neutral but native in spirit: large titles, grouped lists, bottom sheets, pill buttons. The countdown is live.

Radius grows with size

Chips and buttons are pills. Inputs 14px, list groups 16px, cards 20px, sheets 28px. Bigger things get softer corners, like cut stone weathered at the edges.

Two ornaments

The mihrab arch frames the next session, and only the next session. The khatam seal marks a check-in result. Nothing else carries Islamic ornament. The Arabic does the rest.

200ms · ease-out
sheets 350ms · spring-free

Soft, physical motion

Sheets rise, toggles slide, the dragged row lifts. Nothing bounces, nothing fades in on scroll, and the countdown digits just change.

Components

Statuses are soft-filled pills. Each one is a word first and a colour second.

Rules

Do

  • Use Corniche teal for things you can tap or that are selected. Nothing else is teal.
  • Keep the dawn tone for the next session only: the arch, and the presenter banner that leads to it.
  • Group related rows into inset lists. Let space separate groups, not lines.
  • Use the serif only at display sizes (28px+) and for the italic translation.
  • Prefer a sheet over a new screen for anything that takes under a minute.

Don't

  • Don't put icons inside tinted rounded squares. Icons sit bare in teal.
  • Don't use the arch for anything except the next session (and its Eid/special-week variants).
  • Don't use pure white or black, even in dark mode.
  • Don't make points loud. They're information, so show them in terracotta at body weight.
  • Don't add patterns or textures to the background.
